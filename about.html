About Ryan

The boring one, on purpose.

I've spent my career inside banking watching people pay for excitement they didn't need. This site is the antidote.

Why "boring"?

Because the strategies that actually build wealth are dull. Spend less than you earn. Pay down expensive debt first. Buy broad, buy regularly, don't touch it. Nobody sells courses on that — there's no drama in it. So the industry wraps money in jargon and adrenaline instead, and people pay for the wrapping.

I make the boring stuff make sense. Plain language, real numbers, and calculators that let you see the consequences of a decision before you make it.
