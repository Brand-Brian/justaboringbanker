Meet the banker

Thirty years in the room. Here's what actually works.

I'm Ryan Garneau — the Boring Banker. Thirty years in wealth management, twenty-three of them with one of Canada's large wealth management firms, plus a decade teaching Financial Planning at BCIT. This is the class I wish everyone got to take.

The problem nobody teaches

Schools teach trigonometry, the periodic table, and how a bill becomes law. They don't teach what compound interest actually does, how to build a cash flow plan, or how the accounts on your pay stub work. People graduate into the most consequential financial decisions of their lives with zero training.

For thirty years — twenty-three of them inside one of Canada's large wealth management firms, and ten of them teaching Financial Planning at BCIT — I watched smart, hardworking people make expensive decisions. Not because they were careless, but because nobody had ever shown them how money actually works. That's not their fault. It's a gap in how we're taught — and Just a Boring Banker is my attempt to close it.

"The reason I started this website is because schools never taught us how money actually works, and that puts everyday people at a huge disadvantage. After three decades in the industry and the classroom, I want to demystify personal finance so you can make confident decisions. Everyone deserves the tools to build a real plan."
— Ryan Garneau

The banker part

I'm based in Vancouver, British Columbia. Thirty years in wealth management — twenty-three of them at one of Canada's large wealth management firms — teaches you two things. First, the mechanics: markets, accounts, products, taxes, and how they actually fit together. Second, and more valuable: how people behave around money. The panic at the bottoms. The overconfidence at the tops. The quiet power of the clients who did nothing but the boring things, decade after decade, and ended up wealthier than the exciting ones.

That second education is the one this site runs on.

The teacher part

Ten of those thirty years were spent teaching Financial Planning at BCIT — and that taught me something different: the concepts were never hard. They were just never explained plainly. I'm a confident public speaker, and I'm building a speaking tour on the same material this site and the channel cover: the Wealth Building Blocks, explained the way I wish someone had explained them to me at twenty-two.

Why "boring"?

Because the plan that actually works is dull to describe: understand your money flow, take the right actions, protect what you've built, and grow it — then let time do the rest. Nobody sells courses on that; there's no drama in it. So Just a Boring Banker just builds it, one Wealth Building Block at a time — starting with your money flow, because everything after that gets easier once you can actually see it.

What this site is (and isn't)

This is education — the class school skipped, taught with better props: a YouTube series in the making, six machines that let you drive real market history yourself, and a library of plain-language guides. Think Bill Nye for money: genuinely enthusiastic about the unsexy stuff, because raising financial literacy shouldn't feel like homework. It is not financial advice. I don't know your situation, your taxes, or your tolerance for watching a portfolio drop. For decisions, talk to a licensed professional who does.
